Welcome to St. Mary's Kibabii

Founded in 1952 by Catholic missionaries, St. Mary's Kibabii Boys National School (affectionately known as "Kiba") stands as a beacon of academic excellence and character formation in Bungoma County.

Our commitment to holistic education has produced leaders who have excelled in politics, medicine, engineering, academia, and sports. The school's motto, "Orare et Laborare" (Pray and Work), encapsulates our philosophy of balancing spiritual growth with academic rigor.

Why Choose Kiba?

Academic Excellence

Mean score of 8.93 in KCSE 2022. Ranked 85th nationally among 10,000+ schools. Consistent top performer in Bungoma County for over a decade.

KSEF Champions

National gold medals in Energy & Robotics. Represented Kenya at international science fairs. Innovation at our core.

Sports Dynasty

Western Region football champions 2022 & 2023. Rugby 7s, basketball, athletics stars. We produce national athletes.

Catholic Values

Daily mass, chaplaincy programs, strong moral foundation. We build men of character, faith, and integrity.
