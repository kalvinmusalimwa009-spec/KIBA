Privacy Policy

How We Protect Your Information

1. Information We Collect

St. Mary's Kibabii Boys National School collects information that you voluntarily provide to us through our contact form, admissions applications, and newsletter signups, including your name, email address, phone number, and message content.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, process admissions applications, communicate with parents and guardians, and improve our website services. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, analyze site traffic, and remember user preferences. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Google Analytics to analyze website traffic. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not control their data collection practices.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for secure data transmission.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for parents, guardians, and alumni. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Please contact us at info@stmaryskibabii.ac.ke for any privacy-related requests.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated date will be posted at the top of this page. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
