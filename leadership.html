Meet Our Dedicated Team at Kiba

Principal

Dr. Geoffrey Murunga

Chief Principal, St. Mary's Kibabii Boys National School

It is my honor to lead this great institution that has produced outstanding leaders in various fields. At St. Mary's Kibabii (Kiba), we are committed to nurturing young men who excel academically, grow spiritually, and develop into responsible citizens. Our vision is to maintain our position as a center of excellence in the country. Together with my dedicated team of teachers and support staff, we shall continue to uphold the values of "Orare et Laborare" - Pray and Work.

Dr. Geoffrey Murunga Chief Principal, Kiba School
