About Kiba School

Excellence in Education Since 1952

Our History

St. Mary's Kibabii Boys National School, popularly known as "Kiba," was founded in 1952 by Catholic missionaries from the Mill Hill Missionaries. Located in the serene hills of Kibabii, Bungoma County, the school started as a small mission school with just 30 students.

Over the decades, it has grown into one of Kenya's premier national schools, producing leaders in politics, medicine, engineering, academia, and sports. The school's rich heritage combines academic rigor with strong Catholic values.

Our Mission

To provide holistic, quality education that nurtures academic excellence, spiritual growth, and responsible citizenship in a supportive Catholic environment, producing well-rounded young men who are morally upright, intellectually competent, and socially responsible.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in education, producing transformative leaders who serve God and humanity with integrity, compassion, and distinction.

Our Motto

"Orare et Laborare" – Pray and Work. This guiding principle reminds every Kibabiian that success comes from balancing spiritual devotion with hard work and dedication.

Our Core Values

Faith

Rooted in Catholic teachings and daily prayer. We nurture spiritual growth and moral integrity.

Excellence

Pursuit of knowledge and innovation. We strive for the highest standards in everything we do.

Integrity

Character formation and moral uprightness. We build men of honesty and strong principles.

Service

Giving back to society. We believe in community service and helping others.

Guided by our motto, 'Orare et Laborare',
