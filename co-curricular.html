Co-Curricular Excellence

Developing Talents Beyond the Classroom at Kiba

Football

Western Region Champions 2022, 2023. Famous rivalry with Bungoma High School. Produced several national team players including Benard Mang'oli (AFC Leopards).

Music & Drama

National Music Festival finalists 2023, 2024. Top honours in choral verse, instrumental performances, and traditional dances. Award-winning drama team.

Athletics

County champions in 800m, 1500m, and relays. Multiple national qualifiers representing Western Region. State-of-the-art athletics track.

Debate & Public Speaking

Kenya National Debate Council quarter-finalists 2024. Best speaker awards at regional level. Regular participation in Model United Nations.

Chess & Scrabble

Bungoma County chess champions for 3 consecutive years. National qualifiers 2023, 2024. Chess club with 50+ active members.

Community Service

Regular outreach at Kibabii Mission Hospital, environmental clean-ups, and charity drives. Strong partnership with local churches and NGOs.

Clubs & Societies

Science Club

Hands-on experiments, science congress participation, innovation challenges

Computer Club

Coding, web design, robotics, AI workshops, and tech competitions

Literary Club

Creative writing, poetry, book reviews, and journalism

Environmental Club

Tree planting, waste management, conservation awareness campaigns

Christian Union

Prayer meetings, fellowship, retreats, and evangelism outreach

Peace Club

Conflict resolution, peer counseling, anti-bullying campaigns
