KIDA Alumni Association

Kibabiians Development Association

KIDA is the official alumni association of St. Mary's Kibabii Boys National School (Kiba). Our mission is to foster a lifelong connection among Kibabiians, support the development of Kiba, and create opportunities for mentorship and networking.

Membership Benefits: Networking events, mentorship programs, career guidance, annual reunions, and exclusive access to alumni directory.
