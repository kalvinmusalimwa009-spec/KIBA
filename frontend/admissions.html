Admissions Information

Join the Legacy of Excellence at Kiba

Form One Admissions

Admission to St. Mary's Kibabii (Kiba) is through the Ministry of Education's national placement system for National Schools.

  • KCPE cut-off: 400+ marks
  • National school placement
  • Selection from all 47 counties
  • Equal opportunity for all qualified candidates
Download Application Form

Transfer Admissions

Students seeking to transfer to Kiba from other schools are considered based on availability and academic performance.

  • Transfer application form required
  • Previous school reports (last 2 terms)
  • Interview process
  • Limited slots available per class
Transfer Information

Fee Structure

As a public National School, fees at Kiba are regulated by the Ministry of Education.

  • Annual fees: KES 45,000 - 65,000
  • Payment plans available (per term)
  • Bursaries for needy students (apply through county)
  • Scholarships for top performers
