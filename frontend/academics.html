Academic Excellence

Outstanding KCSE Performance & KSEF Achievements at Kiba

KCSE Performance

Mean score of 8.93 (B plain) in 2022. Ranked 85th nationally among 10,000+ schools. Consistent top performer in Bungoma County for 15+ consecutive years.

Science & Innovation

Dominant force at Kenya Science and Engineering Fair (KSEF) with multiple national gold medals in Energy, Robotics, and Environmental Science.

Catholic Values

Holistic education integrating faith, discipline, and community service through Catholic sponsorship and daily spiritual formation.

KSEF Champions

Outstanding National Achievements

  • 2023 - National Champions, Energy Category (Solar-Powered Water Purifier)
  • 2022 - 1st Runners-up, Robotics & Artificial Intelligence
  • 2021 - Best Innovative Project, Western Region (Biogas from Agricultural Waste)
  • 2019 - Gold Medal, Environmental Science & Conservation
  • Represented Bungoma County at National KSEF every year since 2015

Science Fair Innovation Hub

Our state-of-the-art science laboratories are equipped for advanced research and innovation. Students work on cutting-edge projects in renewable energy, robotics, and environmental conservation.

The school has invested in modern equipment including solar energy demonstration kits, robotics programming tools, DNA extraction equipment, and biotechnology lab apparatus valued at over KES 5 million.
